How an Antonio’s pizza comes together

  1. Tossed by hand. Every hand-tossed pie starts as a ball of dough and gets stretched out to 14 or 16 inches.
  2. Sauce, from the middle out. Pizza sauce goes on first. With the pasta, calzones and parm subs, you choose meat sauce or marinara.
  3. A good layer of mozzarella. The Four Cheese adds provolone, cheddar and parmesan. The Margherita uses fresh mozzarella.
  4. Pepperoni, or ten toppings at once. Choose from sausage, peppers, olives, banana peppers, pineapple and more, or get ten on the Deluxe.
  5. Baked till it bubbles. The edges turn golden, the cheese browns in spots and the pepperoni crisps up.
  6. Grab a slice. A hand-tossed cheese slice is $3.00 and a thick-crust Sicilian slice is $3.25. Or take the whole pie home.

A taste of Brooklyn in North Augusta

Antonio’s opened on Georgia Avenue in 2010. Owner Frank grew up in Brooklyn, New York, and brought that taste south with him.

That’s why hand-tossed and Sicilian pies, parm subs, baked pastas and New York cheesecake share one menu with homemade fruit pies. It’s a casual place for lunch and dinner, and when you’re feeding a crowd, we make party pans too.
